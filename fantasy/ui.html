Gandalf Protocol · self-evolving agents

A swarm that learns fantasy football from what actually happened.

Four agents argue over every roster call. A judge scores them on ground truth — real NFL points, not an LLM's opinion. Left alone, a single agent stays flat. The swarm self-evolves and its picks climb. Here it is on a held-out season it never trained on.

The learning curve — top-10 starters' real points

Gandalf swarm (self-evolving) solo agent (naive, no learning)

Each round the swarm adopts the agent whose signal most improves its fit to real outcomes, then refits. Measured out-of-sample on 2025 — nothing tuned to force the shape.

The agents argue — and none of them is right alone

Each agent's own top-5 team scores very differently. The learned swarm (20.6) beats every single one — the combination is the point.

The payoff — the team the swarm drafts vs. the naive team

Same top players, but the naive agent chases recent points into busts (e.g. Rome Odunze, 7.4) the swarm's learned model avoids. Real 2025 rest-of-season points.

How this is honest. Trained on 2022–24, judged on a held-out 2025 season. The metric is real decision quality (top picks' actual points) + prediction error, not a curve we shaped. Ranking the whole player pool is efficient — recent points nearly tie every signal — so the climb is real only because the learned combination beats the naive signal on the decisions that matter. The 4th signal (usage-trend) is marginal and nudges the peak down from +Volume — kept in, not trimmed. One demo domain of the Gandalf swarm; the judge being ground truth is what fantasy adds.
