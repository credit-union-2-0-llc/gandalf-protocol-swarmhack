The Integrity Check

Are they just memorizing 2025? No.

A learning demo is worthless if it peeked at the answers. Three guarantees, each true by construction — not by claim.

1Walk-forward

Every draft reads only weeks 1–3. It’s graded on weeks 4–14 — games that hadn’t happened at decision time. The outcome is never visible when the choice is made.

2Held-out influence

The influence share is four signal weights, fit on the other seasons and never on the one it’s scored against. Four numbers can’t memorize a player’s box score.

3It generalizes

The identical influence weights lift every season they’re tested on. Memorizing one season would do nothing for the others. Strategy transfers; memory doesn’t.
